# Lightweight market scan

**Checked:** 20 November 2026  
**Purpose:** Decide whether a small coding-evaluation task authoring kit is worth testing. This is a search snapshot, not a market-size estimate.

## Visible comparable offers

- [Gumroad: QA Test Plan & UAT Starter Kit](https://distribu0.gumroad.com/l/osrncr) lists a $12.99 price and manual test, UAT, defect, and planning templates. The listing does not disclose sales in the source snapshot.
- [Gumroad: QA CHIP templates pack](https://assurechip.gumroad.com/l/qa-templates-pack) lists a $39 price for multiple QA files, including test cases, defect reporting, and checklists. A listed price is not proof of purchase or conversion.
- [Etsy QA testing category](https://www.etsy.com/market/qa_testing) shows many template listings, with the sampled visible spreadsheet and document products mostly in the low single digits to around $20. Category counts and visible prices change and do not prove sales volume.
- An adjacent [Gumroad coding-interview guide](https://alexcancode.gumroad.com/l/SWEInterviewKit) showed $9.99 and 279 sales in the search snapshot. That is a different product and audience; it cannot establish demand for a task-authoring kit.

## What the scan supports

There are existing digital QA templates, and $7–$12 is a plausible test range for a small, focused digital bundle relative to some visible adjacent offers. The scan did **not** establish sales for a coding-evaluation authoring kit, a differentiated customer need, or likely revenue. The generic QA template category appears crowded; competing on a large volume of generic checklists would be a weak position.

## Low-cost validation before expanding

1. Share the free sample with a small set of people who actually write coding tasks or evaluation benchmarks, and ask what step remains hardest after using it.
2. Track qualified interest and feedback, not likes or page views alone.
3. Test a pay-what-you-want launch only after a real checkout is available; do not describe a hypothetical price as validated.
4. Add further exercises or paid support only if prospective users request them and at least some complete a purchase.

No sales, customer interviews, waitlist, or checkout integration are claimed in this repository.
